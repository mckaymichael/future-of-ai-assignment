# Aesthetic Prompt 
Design a minimalist presentation aesthetic inspired by the atmosphere of a quiet hipster coffee shop on a rainy afternoon. The visual style should feel calm, thoughtful, and reflective, evoking the feeling of sitting near a window while soft jazz plays in the background.

Use a palette dominated by matte earthy tones such as warm beige, muted sand, clay, soft taupe, espresso brown, and light walnut wood textures. These tones should create a grounded, natural foundation across slides and visual elements.

Introduce vibrant greens as subtle accents through plants and organic elements. These greens should vary in tone—from deep forest green to fresh leafy greens—appearing in minimalist plant illustrations, subtle botanical shapes, or natural decorative elements that bring life to the otherwise neutral palette.

The layout should be clean, spacious, and uncluttered, emphasizing generous negative space and calm visual rhythm. Typography should be modern yet warm, with soft rounded or humanist sans-serif fonts paired with simple serif accents that feel artisanal rather than corporate.

Textures should be matte and tactile, suggesting natural materials such as raw wood, recycled paper, linen, ceramic mugs, and handmade surfaces. Avoid glossy or high-tech aesthetics. Instead, favor warmth, authenticity, and subtle imperfection.

**Visual cues may include:**
* soft window light
* rain streaks on glass
* blurred city streets outside a café window
* wooden tables and shelves
* small potted plants and hanging greenery
* notebooks, vinyl records, or coffee cups used sparingly as background motifs

The overall mood should feel contemplative, human, and organic, balancing creativity with introspection. The design should evoke the feeling of quietly reflecting on big ideas while surrounded by warm coffeehouse ambience.

The aesthetic should communicate calm curiosity and thoughtful exploration, maintaining a peaceful and grounded visual tone throughout the entire presentation.
